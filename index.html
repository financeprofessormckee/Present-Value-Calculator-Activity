How to read this experiment

Positive amounts are receipts; negative amounts are outflows. Year 0 is today. The rate is an effective annual rate, compounded once per year. An outline shows a payment's face amount; hatching shows its present value. The shadows at the left add those values on the same dollar scale.

Select a payment in the table or graph. Edit its amount or year in the table, use Earlier/Later, or drag its bar horizontally. Press Escape to cancel a drag. Amounts run from −$10,000 to $10,000 in cents; years from 0 to 30; annual rates from 0 to 25%. All calculations retain full precision.

Some entries need correction. The graph, totals, and formula use the last valid values for those entries.

What happens as the rate changes?

Keep the current amounts and dates fixed. Each point shows the net present value at a different annual rate. Mixed receipts and outflows can make net PV rise, fall, or turn as the rate changes.
