Present Value Visual Calculator

What are future payments worth today? Move them through time, change the interest rate, and watch their present values add up.

Explore the payments

Positive amounts are receipts; negative amounts are outflows. Year 0 means today. Edit the table, or select a payment and use Earlier/Later. You can also drag a bar horizontally to change its date. The rate is an effective annual rate, compounded once per year.

The starting example pays $2,000 today and receives $1,000 in each of the next three years. Compare the discounted receipts with the outflow to find the net present value.

Annual interest rate

Whole years 0–30; annual rate 0–25%. Present value = amount ÷ (1 + rate / 100)year.
